# Chapter 1 - Method

I set three rules before the first night:

1. Walk the same route, in the same direction, at the same time.
2. Use only available light. No flash, no tripod, no lights of my own.
3. Do not stage anything. If a fox does not walk past, there is no fox in the picture.

Rule 2 cost me the most frames. Rule 3 gave me the best ones.

The first two weeks were on black-and-white film. Under sodium lamps every frame came out
with a heavy orange cast on the colour test rolls, and in black and white the lamps blew out
into white blobs. In week three I moved to a digital body, set white balance by hand to
2800K, and kept it there for the rest of the project.
