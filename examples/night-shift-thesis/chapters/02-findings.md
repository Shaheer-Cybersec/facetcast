# Chapter 2 - What the street does at night

Across 42 nights I logged every sound and movement in a notebook next to each frame.

- The bakery on the corner switches on its ovens at about 3:10 AM. The smell reaches the bus depot by 3:30.
- Refuse collection puts the bins out at 3:40 AM; the street gets loud for eleven minutes.
- A fox crossed near number 27 on 31 of the 42 nights.
- Only 9 people walked the full length of the street in six weeks.

The street is not empty. It is working for somebody else: the bakers, the bin crew, the fox.
